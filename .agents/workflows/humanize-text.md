# /humanize-text - Human Voice Editor & Anti-Slop Auditor

$ARGUMENTS

---

## Purpose

Sharpen writing into authentic, human prose without flattening personal voice or cadence. Catches 20+ specific AI slop patterns:
- Binary contrasts ("It's not X. It's Y.")
- Throat-clearing introductions
- Generic importance assertions
- Smooth synonym substitutions
- Abstract noun clusters

---

## Usage

| Command | Action |
|---------|--------|
| `/no-ai-slop <draft>` | Edit draft: make minimum effective edits, preserve cadence/voice, list "What changed". |
| `/no-ai-slop is this slop? <draft>` | Audit mode: list every matched slop pattern with exact quotes without guessing or rewriting. |
| `/no-ai-slop satire <topic>` | Satire mode: draft intentionally exaggerated AI slop for humor. |

---

## Editing Principles

1. **Preserve writer's real voice**: Keep bluntness, cadence, humor, and distinctive rhythm.
2. **Minimum effective edit**: Fix only AI tropes, repetition, and tangled syntax.
3. **Show, don't tell**: Make facts, numbers, and mechanisms do the work.
4. **Portability test**: If a sentence could move unchanged to another person or product, cut it.
